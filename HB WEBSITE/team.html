Our Team

Experienced caregivers. Accountable leaders.

Our leadership brings hands-on healthcare experience — and a shared belief that every person deserves care delivered with dignity, respect, and compassion.

Leadership

The people behind Harmony Bridge Care.

Trust, reliability, and quality of care matter most to families and referring professionals. Our leaders are responsive to the needs of the people we support — and to the staff who support them.

Governance

Clear structure. Shared accountability.

Our governing authorities ensure every part of our operations and services is well managed. They include our Administrative and Management Staff, a Standing Committee with equal agency and community membership, and a Board of Directors that meets Maryland’s COMAR 10.22.02.08 requirements.

  • Board approves the Program Service Plan, Quality Assurance Plan, and annual budget
  • Quarterly financial reporting to the Board
  • Families and the people we support are encouraged to participate
