Who We Are

Care that honors dignity, choice, and the whole person.

Harmony Bridge Care, Inc. is a Bowie-based Maryland provider created to help adults with ID/DD reach their fullest potential — at home and in the community.

We treat each person with dignity, respect, and compassion. Every plan is shaped around the individual’s unique needs, preferences, and goals, so support promotes well-being and independence — never the other way around.

  • Person-centered: every support follows the person’s own Person-Centered Plan (PCP).
  • Family-oriented: open communication and shared decision-making with families and guardians.
  • Standards-driven: built to meet Maryland DDA requirements and COMAR 10.22.

Why Families Choose Us

Quality you can see. Care you can feel.

Maryland sets the reimbursement rates for DDA services, so we compete where it matters most — on the quality and consistency of care.

A small home, by design

Just three people per home means unhurried attention, real relationships, and support that adapts as needs change.

Nurse-led from day one

Our Registered Nurse is involved from admission onward — assessments, care plans, and ongoing supervision of health tasks.

Consistent, familiar staff

Consistent staff assignments mean the people we support see familiar faces who truly know their routines and preferences.

Responsive to families

Fast, personal communication with families, guardians, and Coordinators of Community Services — you'll never be left wondering.

Life in the community

Outings, activities, and friendships beyond the front door, because belonging is part of good health.

Trained & certified team

DDA-required training, CPR and First Aid, and annual refreshers keep our team skilled, confident, and current.
