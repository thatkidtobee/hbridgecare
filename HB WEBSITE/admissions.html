Who We Serve

Support for adults ready to live their best life.

Our services are designed for adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities who want a home in the community with individualized support and nurse-led health oversight.

  • Adults age 18 and older with an intellectual or developmental disability
  • Eligible for services through the Maryland Developmental Disabilities Administration (DDA)
  • Living in, or wishing to live in, the DDA Southern Maryland Region
  • Including people with co-occurring medical or behavioral support needs

FAQ

Questions families often ask.

Who is eligible for Harmony Bridge Care services?

We support adults age 18 and older with intellectual and developmental disabilities who are eligible for Maryland DDA waiver services. Children’s residential services are licensed separately and are not part of our programs.

How are services paid for?

Our services are Maryland DDA waiver services, authorized through each person’s Person-Centered Plan and funded through Medicaid. Residents of our group home contribute toward room and board. Your Coordinator of Community Services can help explain what’s available to you.

What is a Person-Centered Plan (PCP)?

A PCP is an individualized plan, developed with the person and the people who know them best, that describes their goals, preferences, strengths, and the supports they need. Every service we provide follows the person’s PCP.

How many people live in the home?

Our home is a single-family house for three people. Each person has a private, furnished bedroom, and shares comfortable common areas, a kitchen, and outdoor space.

Is there a nurse involved in care?

Yes. A DDA-approved Registered Nurse leads health assessments, contributes to each PCP, and delegates and supervises health-related tasks. Each person is scheduled for at least two physical and dental examinations each year.

Can families visit and stay involved?

Absolutely. We see families and guardians as partners. We encourage visits, invite family participation in planning, and regularly ask how we’re doing.

Can residents attend day programs or work?

Yes. We coordinate with local day and employment service providers and provide transportation and travel training, so each person can pursue the activities and goals in their plan.

Where are you located and when do you open?

Our administrative office is in Bowie, Prince George’s County. We are applying for Maryland DDA provider approval and OHCQ licensing, with our first home planned to open in 2027. We welcome inquiries now so we can begin planning together.

Still have questions?

We’re here Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM, with on-call coverage 24 hours a day.
