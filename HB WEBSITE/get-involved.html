Your Impact

Where your support goes.

Fundraising provides supplemental income that helps us maintain the quality of care, retain the best-qualified staff, and offer programs that meet each person’s needs and preferences.

  • Building an operating reserve that protects services during payment delays and emergencies
  • Special programs and activities for the people we support
  • Equipment, technology, and software that improve daily life and care
  • Community services and programs that grow independence
