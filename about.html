About Harmony Bridge Care

Built on dignity. Guided by each person’s goals.

We’re a Maryland provider created to help adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities live full, self-directed lives in the communities of their choice.

Our Story

Creating programs that stand out — because people deserve more.

Harmony Bridge Care, Inc. (HBC) is a Maryland corporation headquartered in Bowie, Prince George’s County, established to partner with the Developmental Disabilities Administration (DDA) in supporting adults age 18 and older with ID/DD.

Our founders bring hands-on healthcare experience as a Certified Nursing Assistant and a Certified Medication Technician, joined by a Registered Nurse who leads health oversight. Together, we’re building a provider that families can trust — focused on supporting each person to set goals and take part in the decisions that affect their lives.

Our model is intentionally simple: a small, family-style home; consistent staff; nurse-led health care; and fast, personal communication with families and Coordinators of Community Services.

Our Mission

To develop and implement unique service programs and activities that stand out from other providers — assuring quality care and services that help persons with ID/DD achieve their maximum potential.

Our Vision

To be the most sought-after and preferred agency providing services to persons with ID/DD throughout the State of Maryland and beyond — by rendering value to the persons we serve and all our stakeholders.

Our Goals

What person-centered support looks like in practice.

We deliver comprehensive support that meets the physical, emotional, and social needs of each person — including people with co-occurring medical or behavioral support needs.

Enhancing Quality of Life

Promoting independence, belonging, emotional well-being, social connection, and personal fulfillment.

Personalized Care

Respecting personal choices, cultural backgrounds, and values so care aligns with each person’s wishes.

Safety & Well-being

A safe, secure home with the right safety measures and healthy living conditions in place.

Health & Independence

Medication support, coordinated appointments, healthy lifestyle choices, and access to medical care.

Activities of Daily Living

Support with bathing, dressing, grooming, meals, and housekeeping — while building independence.

Social Integration

Outings, activities, and community engagement that foster belonging and reduce isolation.

Family Partnership

Open communication, shared decision-making, and collaboration with families and guardians.

Continuity of Care

Clear care plans, documented preferences, and coordinated, consistent communication across the team.

Standards & Oversight

Accountable to the highest standards in Maryland.

Everything we do is designed around the requirements of Maryland’s Developmental Disabilities Administration and the Code of Maryland Regulations (COMAR 10.22), with clear governance and regular quality review.

  • Governing Board of Directors approves our Program Service Plan, Quality Assurance Plan, and budget.
  • Standing Committee with equal agency and community representation.
  • Quality Assurance Team chaired by our CEO, with regular family and participant feedback.
  • Sound fiscal controls including monthly reconciliation and quarterly Board financial review.
